# Staged movement pack

Source: src/engine/staged-moves.js. Trigger Power up, Energy charge and Beam release directly; use More moves for the other nine. Auto chooses complete staged actions.

The existing 21-bone Meshy rig has wrist joints but no finger joints: hand placement/orientation is animated, finger clenching is not. Coordinates are metres in root space; angles are radians. Head, shoulders and elbow poles have separate channels. No generated replacement character or paid service is used.
