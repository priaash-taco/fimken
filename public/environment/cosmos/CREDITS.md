# Cosmic environment assets

`lunar-color.png` and `lunar-normal.png`: NASA Scientific Visualization Studio, CGI Moon Kit, Ernie Wright / Noah Petro; LRO LROC color mosaic and LOLA elevation data. Public-domain source data.
Source: https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4720/
Downloaded as 2048 x 1024 PNG derivatives from https://github.com/molczane/MoonExplorer/tree/main/shared/src/commonMain/composeResources/files/textures (moon_albedo_2k.png and moon_normal_2k.png). This mirror was used because the NASA file host was unavailable. The normal map was baked from ldem_16_uint.tif; its south-positive green channel is inverted in the Three.js material.

`solar-photosphere.jpg`: original deterministic artwork generated by scripts/bake-solar-detail.py, not a solar observation. Fine granulation and stylized sunspot groups.
`nebula-stars.jpg`: original artwork generated by scripts/build-cosmos.py.
The celestial sizes and distances are cinematic, not astronomical scale.
